# AC AMC Dubai – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

AC AMC Dubai provides air conditioning cleaning, maintenance, servicing and repair solutions for residential and commercial customers in Dubai, UAE. Its services also include preventive maintenance and Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC).

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating the company's core services clearly, establishing credible brand messaging and making it easier for homeowners, property managers and businesses to request assistance.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Hero Positioning and Service Messaging
- Replaced the broad headline with a more direct introduction to AC cleaning, maintenance and repair services.
- Made the company's main service offering immediately understandable.
- Added direct enquiry options to help visitors take the next step.

### 2. More Consistent and Credible Business Information
- Removed conflicting experience claims of 20+ years and 15+ years.
- Avoided repeating unsupported figures such as 100+ certified team members and 230+ completed projects.
- Focused the credibility messaging on the company's published services and identified technical team members.

### 3. Improved Customer Journey and Enquiry Access
- Introduced service-specific enquiry links for more relevant customer enquiries.
- Added clearer process information to explain the service journey.
- Made contact options and mobile conversion buttons more accessible.
- Included dedicated sections for AMC enquiries and frequently asked questions.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual presentation:** Used original CSS artwork for the hero and technical visuals instead of relying on potentially unreliable external photography.
- **Page structure:** Organised the homepage into clear sections covering services, technical expertise, team members, process, AMC, reviews, FAQs and contact.
- **Service discovery:** Presented the six main service categories in a more structured format.
- **User experience:** Improved navigation and made important enquiry actions easier to find.
- **Consistency:** Used clearer, more consistent messaging throughout the homepage and avoided unsupported numerical claims.
- **Responsive conversion:** Included accessible mobile enquiry buttons to support visitors using smaller screens.
